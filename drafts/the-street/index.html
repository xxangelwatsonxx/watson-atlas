Built for Awaab's Law

Resonest

Built for Awaab's Law, Resonest is a privacy-first digital twin that helps social landlords get ahead of damp, mould and cold homes.

Picture every home on your street lit up: warm where help is needed first, calm where all is well.

  • Uses existing property and weather data, with no in-home sensors or tenant monitoring
  • Shows which homes will struggle first in winter, and why
  • Highlights which low-cost improvements will make homes safer and cheaper to keep warm
  • Helps teams meet Awaab's Law with confidence, with clear action lists and deadlines for every home
  • Keeps an audit trail of what was done and what changed
Housing compliance

SiteOps

A housing compliance platform for capturing inspections on site and turning them into consistent, branded PDF reports, tied to the right property. It works alongside a landlord's existing housing system, rather than replacing it.

A repairs and maintenance module is on the SiteOps roadmap.

  • On-site inspection capture and branded PDF reports for fire risk assessments, electrical work, and building and tower-block surveys
  • A property register keyed on UPRN, with EPC and condition information
  • A no-code form and report designer
  • Job planning to schedule, assign and track inspection programmes
  • A mobile app for on-site capture against the same forms

The rest of the terrace

Five supporting capabilities that sit around Resonest and SiteOps, each one a home on the same street.

Building safety & cladding reporting

A specialist reporting system for building-safety work, including FRAEW, desktop assessments, EWS1 and site-visit reports, with configurable forms, specialist PDFs, job management and a connected surveyor app.

NO. 4

Field & surveyor app

A mobile app, in live use, for surveyors and assessors to capture photographs, geotagged data and evidence on site, even offline, against the same forms used in the web platform.

NO. 5

Property data

UPRN address verification, EPC certificate lookup and weather data. These already underpin Resonest and the SiteOps property register.

NO. 6

Integrations

Already have a housing or asset system? We connect rather than replace it, exchanging records, results, reports and actions using UPRN as the common reference.

NO. 7

Bespoke housing software

When off-the-shelf software does not quite fit, we design around the requirement, with controlled integration into the systems you already use.
