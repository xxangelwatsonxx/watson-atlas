What we do

Software that helps housing teams care for homes and communities

We build around the way your organisation works, connecting data, people and processes so every home gets the attention it deserves.

Asset & stock data

Bring your homes, components and condition data into one clear, trusted picture, so planning and investment decisions start from a shared view of every property.

Repairs & maintenance workflows

Simple, joined-up journeys from the first report to the finished job, built around how your residents, contact centre, surveyors and contractors actually work.

Compliance tracking

Keep gas, electrical, fire, water, lifts and damp & mould checks visible and on schedule, with clear evidence trails and early sight of what is coming up next.

Digital twins of homes & estates

Interactive digital representations of buildings and neighbourhoods that help teams explore options, test plans and communicate change before work begins.

Applied AI

Practical AI for everyday housing tasks: reading documents, sorting and routing requests, spotting patterns in data. Always with people in the loop.

Bespoke software & integrations

When off-the-shelf software does not quite fit, we design around the requirement and connect it with the systems you already rely on.

How we work

Clear steps, shared goals, lasting partnerships

Every engagement follows a simple, transparent path, so you always know where things stand and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Define

    We start with people: residents, users, workflows, integrations and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    Narrow, early versions on representative data, so everyone can see and shape the direction.

  3. 03

    Build

    Delivery in stages, with source control, automated testing and staging environments.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Careful checks against agreed acceptance criteria before anything goes live.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    A smooth handover into everyday use, with ongoing support as needs grow.
