## What we do

**Heading:** Software that helps housing teams care for homes and communities

**Intro:** We design around how your organisation works. Resonest and SiteOps sit at the centre, with building-safety reporting, a field app, property data, integrations and bespoke software around them.

### Resonest

Built for Awaab's Law, Resonest is a privacy-first digital twin that helps social landlords get ahead of damp, mould and cold homes.

- Uses existing property and weather data, with no in-home sensors or tenant monitoring
- Shows which homes will struggle first in winter, and why
- Highlights which low-cost improvements will make homes safer and cheaper to keep warm
- Helps teams meet Awaab's Law with confidence, with clear action lists and deadlines for every home
- Keeps an audit trail of what was done and what changed

### SiteOps

A housing compliance platform for capturing inspections on site and turning them into consistent, branded PDF reports, tied to the right property. It works alongside a landlord's existing housing system, rather than replacing it.

- On-site inspection capture and branded PDF reports for fire risk assessments, electrical work, and building and tower-block surveys
- A property register keyed on UPRN, with EPC and condition information
- A no-code form and report designer
- Job planning to schedule, assign and track inspection programmes
- A mobile app for on-site capture against the same forms

A repairs and maintenance module is on the SiteOps roadmap.

### Around Resonest and SiteOps

**Building safety & cladding reporting**  
A specialist operational and reporting system for building-safety work, including Fire Risk Appraisal of External Walls (FRAEW), desktop assessments, EWS1 and site-visit reports, with configurable forms, specialist PDFs, job management and a connected surveyor app.

**Field & surveyor app**  
A mobile app, in live use, for surveyors and assessors to capture inspection evidence on site, including photographs, geotagged data and offline capture, against the same structured forms used in the web platform.

**Property data**  
UPRN address verification, EPC certificate lookup and import from the register, and weather data. These already underpin Resonest and the SiteOps property register, including fabric and condition information on each home.

**Integrations**  
When a landlord already has a housing or asset system, we connect rather than replace it. Property records, inspection results, generated reports and remedial actions can be exchanged, using UPRN as the common property reference.

**Bespoke housing software**  
When off-the-shelf software does not quite fit, we design around the requirement, including specialist operational systems and controlled integration with the systems you already use.
